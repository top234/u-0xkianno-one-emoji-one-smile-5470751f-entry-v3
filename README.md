Entry for the [One Emoji, One Smile](https://make.fun/bounty/u-0xkianno-one-emoji-one-smile-5470751f) competition on make.fun.

`main.ts` writes exactly one emoji — 😄 (U+1F604, Grinning Face with Smiling Eyes) — and nothing else, no newline, to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

Why 😄: a genuine smile aimed straight at a stranger — warm at a glance, universal, clearly chosen rather than the default smiley 🙂/😊, and friendly with no touch or intimacy. An upgrade over v1 🤗 and v2 🌞.
